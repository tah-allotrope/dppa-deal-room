DPPA Deal Room

A role-play. Each table is the internal DPPA working group of one factory, and each participant plays one department.

The group works from the first eligibility check to the first settlement.

EligibilityTerm sheetContractRegistration

One table, one factory

  • 4–5 tables, 6–7 people per table.
  • Each table gets one factory profile, one set of role cards and one set of contracting cards.
  • Two Allotrope facilitators play the CFO throughout. Other tables can ask questions.
  • With more tables, two tables share a factory.

A Apparel

Inside an industrial park

Buys electricity from the park developer, not EVN. Single day shift, six days a week. The brand wants more renewable electricity each year.

B Footwear

Direct EVN customer

Standalone site with its own EVN contract. Two shifts, six days a week. The brand has set a supplier target for renewable electricity by 2030.

C Electronics components

Inside an industrial park

Buys electricity from the park developer. Runs mostly on weekdays, with much lower load at weekends. The parent company has an RE100 target.

D Electronics assembly

Direct EVN customer

Large standalone site with its own EVN contract. Runs 24/7 with a steady load. The parent has an RE100 target and customers ask for lower-carbon products.

Round 3: Pitch to the CFO

15 min

One presenter per group, 60 seconds. The room votes for the pitch the CFO should approve.

Why now?

RE100 or customer requirement.

What we get

Price certainty on X% of load.

Costs and risks

Projected cost vs EVN tariff, curtailment, a 10–20 year commitment.

What we need from you

Approval for load data work, a non-binding term sheet, HQ sign-off.

Facilitators and the room play the CFO. Brace yourselves.

THE CFO
Hard question 1Is this cheaper than EVN, and in which years?
Hard question 2What if the solar farm is curtailed or goes bankrupt?
Hard question 3Does this count as a derivative on our balance sheet?
Hard question 4Why sign for 15 years when the regulation is still new?
Hard question 5Can HQ just buy I-RECs instead?

Now the room votes: which pitch does the CFO approve?
